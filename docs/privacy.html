Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Overview & Local-First Design

BitChord Desktop is a free, open-source desktop music player licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3). The application is designed to run locally on your computer without centralized tracking servers, behavioral telemetry, or advertising SDKs.

Your playback preferences, local playlists, listening history, and Replay statistics are stored locally on your device using standard operating system storage.

2. Google Drive Backup & User Data

If you choose to connect Google Drive under Settings > Backup and Restore, BitChord Desktop uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE to access only the following limited Google API scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — Provides access solely to a hidden application-specific folder in your Google Drive. BitChord Desktop cannot view, read, modify, or delete any of your personal Google Drive files or folders outside that hidden app folder.
  • Basic Google account metadata (email address and display name from Google Drive About) is displayed inside the app so you know which Google account is connected.

Backup files uploaded to your Google Drive app folder contain compressed JSON snapshots of your non-credential app settings, local playlists, liked/disliked track IDs, playback history, and monthly Replay listening statistics. Account cookies, passwords, and third-party API tokens are strictly excluded from backups.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

BitChord Desktop's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Google user data accessed via Drive App Data is used exclusively to provide backup, restore, and Replay synchronization features directly requested by you inside BitChord Desktop.
  • BitChord Desktop does not transfer, sell, or share your Google user data with third parties, advertisers, or AI/ML training pipelines.
  • OAuth tokens remain encrypted or stored locally on your machine (using Windows DPAPI on Windows and local OS preferences on Linux/macOS) and are never sent to the maintainer's servers.

4. Custom OAuth (BYO) & Data Control

For maximum transparency, BitChord Desktop also supports Bring Your Own (BYO) Google Cloud Desktop OAuth Client IDs and direct access tokens, allowing you to use your own Google Cloud project instead of the built-in client.

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time inside Settings > Backup and Restore > Google Drive backup (which revokes the token and clears local credentials), delete individual cloud backups in-app, or remove the hidden app data directly from Google Drive via Settings > Manage apps.

5. Third-Party Services

When you use optional music and metadata features, BitChord Desktop communicates directly from your computer with the third-party services you choose to enable:

  • YouTube / YouTube Music for catalog search, stream resolution, and optional account library sync.
  • Lyrics providers (LRCLIB, Musixmatch, YouTube) and optional scrobbling/presence services (Last.fm, ListenBrainz, Discord local IPC, Spotify Canvas).
  • Listen Together party servers (default community relay or self-hosted/Cloudflare Quick Tunnel) only when you explicitly join or host a party session.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to report a privacy concern, please contact the maintainer at me@efzyn.my.id or open an issue on GitHub.
