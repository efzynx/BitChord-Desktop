1. PRIVACY POLICY SUMMARY
Full Privacy Policy: https://bitchord-desktop.efzyn.my.id/privacy.html

- Local-First Architecture: BitChord Desktop runs directly on your device without a centralized developer tracking backend. Listening history, playlists, preferences, lyrics cache, and Replay statistics stay locally on your computer.
- Third-Party Services You Enable:
  * YouTube Music / InnerTube APIs: Used for streaming audio, search, and synchronized account library features when signed in.
  * Google Drive Backup (Optional): If you connect Google Drive, BitChord Desktop uses the restricted "drive.appdata" scope solely to store and restore your backup files inside your private hidden App Data folder. No developer server stores your Google Drive tokens.
  * Lyrics & Integrations (Optional): Requests sent to LRCLIB, Musixmatch, Last.fm, Discord RPC (local IPC), or Listen Together party servers only occur when used or enabled.
- No Ads & No Sale of Data: BitChord Desktop contains zero advertisements and never sells or monetizes personal data.
